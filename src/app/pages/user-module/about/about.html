Our mission

To make the divine feel close enough to hear.

We began with a simple wish: to offer a place where the heart can rest, the voice can sing and every guest is received as family.

Here, bhajan is not performance and seva is not obligation. Each is a way of turning attention toward love — gently, repeatedly, with care for every detail.

Our vision

Ancient wisdom, held with contemporary grace.

We imagine an ashram that feels both timeless and alive — where sacred music, quiet study, flower offerings and community care belong to the same rhythm.

Beauty is not decoration here. It is an invitation to slow down, notice and remember what the heart already knows.

The living practice

Darshan, kirtan and seva as one unbroken melody.

Spiritual philosophy

Love is the simplest form of knowing.

Our practice follows the path of bhakti: attention offered again and again until the ordinary becomes luminous.

The flute, the lamp and the shared meal are not separate lessons. Each asks the same question — can we be fully present with the divine and with one another?
